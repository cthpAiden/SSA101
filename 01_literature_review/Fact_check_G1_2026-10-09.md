## 1. Verified, safe to use

| Source | Fact | Where in the paper |

| Giletta et al. (2021) | 233 effect sizes from 60 longitudinal studies on 4 continents. Peer influence β̄ = .08: small but significant and robust. Same size across behaviours and ages. Stronger over shorter time lags and when peers are measured outside the classroom. | Abstract |
| Auerbach et al. (2018) | 13,984 full-time first-year students, 19 colleges, 8 countries. 35.3% screened positive for a lifetime disorder, 31.4% for a 12-month disorder. 12-month: major depression 18.5%, generalized anxiety 16.7%, alcohol use disorder 6.3%, panic 4.5%. RPI scale: 10 statement pairs, Cronbach's alpha .70 to .76. Females scored higher overall. | Abstract, Method, Results |
| Borsari & Carey (2003) | 23 studies, 102 tests, 53,825 participants. Students overestimate how much peers drink and how much peers approve of drinking. Overall Z Fisher = .342 (medium). Descriptive norms .291 (65 tests), injunctive norms .433 (37 tests). Gap larger for women, distant reference groups, vague questions and smaller campuses. | Abstract, Results |
| Xiong et al. (2026) | 317 first-year students in China (71.8% female, mean age 18.16), daily reports for 30 days. Social media use intensity and social media use problems on one day predicted worse interpersonal adaptation the next day. Upward social comparison mediated the link from social media use problems to adaptation. | Abstract |
| Walton & Cohen (2011) | Randomized controlled trial, N = 92 (49 African-American, 43 European-American). One brief session framed early adversity as common and short-lived, and students wrote the message themselves. Over 3 years it raised African-American students' GPA and halved the minority achievement gap. Better self-reported health, fewer doctor visits. | Abstract |
| Nguyen et al. (2024) | 409 first-year medical students, University of Medicine and Pharmacy at Ho Chi Minh City, Nov to Dec 2020, anonymous survey, 97% participation. Two-thirds reported moderate or high stress (8.8% high). Stress sources on a 1 to 4 scale: future competence/endurance 3.02, academic workload 2.87, financial concerns 2.65. Self-distraction was the most used coping strategy (2.80), seeking social support 2.62. | Abstract, Table 1, Results, Fig 1 |
| Festinger (1954) | Human Relations, 7(2), 117-140. People evaluate their abilities and opinions by comparing themselves with others. | Crossref |
| APA Dictionary | Social comparison theory lists three types: upward, downward and lateral. | dictionary.apa.org/social-comparison-theory |

## 3. True, but say the limits if a board asks

- Giletta: children and adolescents, not university students.
- Borsari & Carey: drinking norms, not academic comparison.
- Auerbach: self-report screening, not clinical diagnosis. 19 colleges chosen by convenience in 8 mostly high-income countries, 45.5% response rate, no Asian country.
- Xiong: students in China, 71.8% female.
- Nguyen: one medical school, data from late 2020 (COVID period).
- Walton & Cohen: a US college sample; the effect was shown for African-American students.
- β̄ = .08 and Z = .342 are different statistics from different studies. Putting them side by side is our argument, not a measured result.

Power figures in the speaker notes (alpha .05, two-tailed, 80% power): a correlation of r = .20 needs about 194 people; a paired t-test with d = .20 needs about 199; with 120 people the smallest detectable r is about .25.
